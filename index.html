Hollow Bay: When good solutions create new problems

You are the coastal management team. Over 20 simulated years, protect fish stocks, sustain community wellbeing, and manage a limited public budget. You will make four decisions, one every five years. Your decisions have delayed effects, feedback loops, and unintended consequences—and the system will surprise you.

Your mission

Manage the whole system—not just the visible problem.

Hollow Bay depends on fishing, coastal habitats, and local jobs. Fish catches have been falling. Your team will make one decision per round, then receive new information about the consequences.

Starting situation
Fish stocks are under pressure. The community wants action now, while ecological recovery may take years. You have a budget of 100 units.

What you will practise

Feedback loopsDelaysBounded rationalitySystem boundariesArchetypesUnintended consequences

How to play

  1. Read the scenario and choose one intervention.
  2. Record what you expect to happen.
  3. Advance the year and reveal consequences.
  4. Use the debrief to explain surprises with systems concepts.

There is no perfect option. Your score is a prompt for discussion, not a scientific forecast.
